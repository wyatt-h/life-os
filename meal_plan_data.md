## WEEK A: Chicken & Salmon Focus

### Breakfast — Quick Oats & Egg Scramble
- Time: 15 mins
- Ingredients: 3 whole eggs, 1 cup liquid egg whites, 1 tsp olive oil, 1.5 cups instant oatmeal, 1 tbsp almond butter, 1/2 cup blueberries
- Instructions:
  1. Microwave oats with water for 2 minutes.
  2. Heat olive oil in pan over medium heat.
  3. Scramble eggs and egg whites together in pan for 3 minutes.
  4. Top oats with almond butter and blueberries.
  5. Serve eggs alongside oats.
- Nutrition: 650 kcal | 45g Pro | 65g Carb | 22g Fat

### Lunch — Air-Fried Teriyaki Chicken & Sweet Potato
- Time: 2 min at office (prepped Sunday)
- Ingredients: 6 oz chicken breast (cubed), Soyaki sauce, 1.5 cups diced sweet potatoes, 1 cup broccoli florets
- Sunday Prep Instructions:
  1. Wash and dice 3 lbs sweet potatoes into 1-inch cubes. Toss with 1 tbsp EVOO, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, black pepper.
  2. Air fry sweet potatoes at 400°F for 20–25 minutes in batches, shaking every 10 min.
  3. Cut 2.5 lbs chicken breast into bite-sized cubes. Toss with 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 3 tbsp Soyaki sauce.
  4. Air fry chicken at 380°F for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway. Internal temp 165°F.
  5. Microwave 3 bags TJ's Frozen Broccoli Florets per package instructions.
  6. Divide into 5 containers: ~1.5 cups sweet potatoes, ~6 oz chicken, ~1 cup broccoli. Refrigerate. Good 5 days.
- At Office: Microwave for 2 minutes.
- Nutrition: 700 kcal | 55g Pro | 95g Carb | 10g Fat

### Dinner — 15-Minute Salmon & Rice Bowl
- Time: 15 mins
- Ingredients: 6 oz salmon fillet, 2 cups Jasmine rice (microwave pouch), 1/2 avocado, 1 tbsp soy sauce / 生抽
- Instructions:
  1. Air-fry salmon fillet at 400°F for 12 minutes.
  2. Microwave Jasmine rice pouch (~90 seconds).
  3. Slice avocado.
  4. Assemble bowl: rice base, salmon on top, avocado slices, drizzle soy sauce.
- Nutrition: 850 kcal | 45g Pro | 100g Carb | 30g Fat

## WEEK B: Turkey & Beef Focus

### Breakfast — Avocado Toast & Protein
- Time: 15 mins
- Ingredients: 3 slices TJ's Whole Wheat Sandwich Bread, 1/2 avocado, 4 whole eggs
- Instructions:
  1. Toast 3 slices of whole wheat bread.
  2. Mash 1/2 avocado with a fork, season with salt and pepper.
  3. Spread mashed avocado on toast.
  4. Fry or scramble 4 eggs in a pan with a little olive oil.
  5. Serve eggs alongside avocado toast.
- Nutrition: 700 kcal | 35g Pro | 60g Carb | 35g Fat

### Lunch — Ground Turkey & Pasta Skillet
- Time: 2 min at office (prepped Sunday)
- Ingredients: 6 oz Jennie-O 93% lean ground turkey, 2 cups TJ's Organic Whole Wheat Penne, 1/2 cup TJ's Tomato Basil Marinara, 1 cup green beans
- Sunday Prep Instructions:
  1. Bring large pot of salted water to boil. Cook 1 lb TJ's Organic Whole Wheat Penne per package (10–12 mins). Drain.
  2. In large pan over medium-high heat, cook 2.5 lbs Jennie-O ground turkey. Break apart as it cooks. Season with 1 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp onion powder, 1/2 tsp salt, black pepper. Cook until no pink (~10 mins).
  3. Add cooked pasta to turkey pan. Pour in 2.5 cups TJ's Tomato Basil Marinara. Stir and heat 3–4 minutes.
  4. Steam green beans in microwave per package.
  5. Divide into 5 containers. Refrigerate. Good 5 days.
- At Office: Microwave for 2 minutes.
- Nutrition: 750 kcal | 50g Pro | 90g Carb | 18g Fat

### Dinner — Quick Beef & Rice Stir-Fry
- Time: 15 mins
- Ingredients: 6 oz Sprouts 90/10 ground beef, 2 cups Jasmine rice (microwave pouch), 1 cup TJ's Stir Fry Vegetables (frozen), 1 tbsp Kadoya sesame oil, 1 tbsp soy sauce / 生抽
- Instructions:
  1. Brown ground beef in pan over medium-high heat for 7 minutes, breaking apart.
  2. Add frozen stir fry vegetables directly to pan, cook 5 minutes stirring occasionally.
  3. Microwave Jasmine rice pouch (~90 seconds).
  4. Serve beef and veggie mix over rice.
  5. Drizzle sesame oil and soy sauce over top.
- Nutrition: 800 kcal | 45g Pro | 95g Carb | 25g Fat
